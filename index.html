ABOUT ME

Artificial Intelligence Researcher

I am an Artificial Intelligence researcher with an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from Razi University. My research focuses on developing efficient and intelligent machine learning systems, with particular interests in deep learning, computer vision, signal processing, and lightweight neural network design.

My recent research has focused on knowledge distillation, model compression, and efficient deep learning architectures, particularly for cardiovascular disease diagnosis using ECG signals and images.

I am interested in developing reliable and computationally efficient AI models that can be deployed in resource-constrained, low-power, and edge devices.
