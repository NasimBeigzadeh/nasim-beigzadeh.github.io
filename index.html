ABOUT ME

Artificial Intelligence Researcher & Graduate Researcher

I am an Artificial Intelligence researcher with a strong interest in developing efficient and reliable deep learning methods for real-world biomedical applications.

My research focuses on medical imaging, biomedical signal processing, deep learning, and knowledge distillation, with particular emphasis on cardiovascular disease diagnosis from ECG signals and developing lightweight models for resource-constrained environments.

My broader interests include computer vision, model compression, intelligent systems, and the development of practical AI solutions for healthcare.
