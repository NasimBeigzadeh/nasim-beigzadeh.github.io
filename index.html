ABOUT

About Me

I am an AI researcher with more than four years of research experience at the Signal and Image Processing Laboratory at Razi University.

My research focuses on medical imaging, biomedical signal processing, and deep learning for healthcare applications, with a particular emphasis on cardiovascular disease diagnosis using ECG signals.

I am particularly interested in designing lightweight and computationally efficient neural networks through knowledge distillation and model compression, with the goal of developing AI systems suitable for resource-constrained and wearable healthcare devices.
