About Me

I am an Artificial Intelligence researcher with an M.Sc. background in Computer Engineering and Artificial Intelligence from Razi University. My research interests lie at the intersection of deep learning, biomedical signal processing, and healthcare applications.

My recent work has focused on ECG-based cardiovascular disease classification, knowledge distillation, and designing computationally efficient neural networks. I am particularly interested in methods that reduce model complexity while preserving predictive performance, making AI models more practical for resource-constrained healthcare settings.

I have collaborated with researchers affiliated with Radboud University in the Netherlands and have worked on deep learning approaches for biomedical signals and ECG representations. My broader research interests include reliable AI for healthcare, medical image analysis, and efficient machine learning.

I am interested in pursuing doctoral research that connects rigorous AI methodology with meaningful healthcare applications, including the development of efficient, robust, and human-centred AI systems.
